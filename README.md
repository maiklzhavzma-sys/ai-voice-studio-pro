**AI Voice Studio (AiVS)** is a professional, standalone desktop workstation designed for personal vocal training, practice, analysis, and next-generation AI voice modification. 

Unlike cloud-based services, AI Voice Studio operates **100% locally on your machine**. Your audio files, vocal recordings, and trained models never leave your computer, ensuring absolute privacy and zero latency.

---

Key Features in v1.5.0

* **Pure Offline Inference:** Run heavy AI voice processing models locally via an isolated environment without any internet dependency.
* **DAW-Style Workflow:** Enjoy features built for musicians, including seamless audio transport, custom region selections, and instant rendering.
* **Smart Device Routing:** Direct hardware address support (`setSinkId`) lets you switch between monitors, headphones, or professional audio interfaces on the fly.
* **Session Management (.avs):** Save your entire workflow—including model paths, audio sources, and custom presets—into single, native project containers.
* **Real-Time UI Feedback:** Track your AI rendering with a precise "Neural Audio Processing" visual progress bar and countdown timer.

System Requirements

To ensure smooth local rendering, your system should meet the following requirements:
* **macOS:** Apple Silicon (M1/M2/M3/M4 chips) or modern Intel Macs.
* **Windows:** Windows 10/11 with an NVIDIA GPU (CUDA support recommended for faster rendering).
* **Storage:** Solid State Drive (SSD) is highly recommended for faster model loading.

## 📱 Ecosystem: AiVS PRO Remote (iOS)

<p align="center">
  <img src="https://www.aivs.pro/images/front/EN_05.png" width="35%" alt="AiVS PRO Remote Main Interface">
</p>

Take your vocal recordings out of the noisy computer room. **AiVS PRO Remote** turns your iPhone or iPad into a wireless, hardware-style pocket controller and standalone recorder that syncs directly with your desktop studio over Wi-Fi.

* **Zero-Configuration Link:** Automatic desktop discovery within your local Wi-Fi network—no accounts or internet required.
* **Vocal Booth Isolation:** Record your vocals in a quiet room on your phone, crop the wave, and hit `SEND` to instantly beam the audio straight into the desktop studio's INPUT.
* **Wireless Touch Control:** Remotely tweak sliders (`Cluster`, `Pitch`, `Threshold`), switch target vocal models, and trigger the heavy AI `CONVERT` process while standing at the microphone.
* **Safety Lockout:** The mobile interface automatically dims and locks inputs during active neural rendering to protect your session settings from accidental touch.
